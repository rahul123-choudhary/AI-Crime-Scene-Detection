1. Authorized Law Enforcement Use Only

This platform constitutes an official law enforcement diagnostic tool. Unauthorized access, account spoofing, credential sharing, or extraction of crime scene media outside official prosecutorial duties is strictly prohibited and subject to criminal prosecution under federal and state statutes.

  • Users must possess active, verified official police, laboratory, or judicial credentials.
  • Credentials and session cookies are non-transferable and tied to registered officer badge numbers.
  • Attempted security tampering triggers automated forensic audit logs and IP lockout.

2. Neural Detection & Human-in-the-Loop Standard

While the YOLOv8 and Dual Specialist computer vision engines demonstrate industry-leading precision in weapon localization and evidence tagging, neural classifications constitute probabilistic investigative aids:

  • All bounding box detections and threat score metrics must be verified by a qualified human investigator.
  • The system is designed to accelerate evidence cataloging, not replace certified forensic testimony.
  • Court-admissible PDF reports must be reviewed and signed by the assigned case investigator.

3. Chain of Custody & Evidentiary Integrity

Digital files ingested into the platform receive automated SHA-256 digital digests recorded in tamper-evident audit logs. Investigators agree not to introduce fabricated or altered media intended to mislead criminal proceedings.

4. Account Termination & Case Archival

Agencies retain the right to archive or expunge case records and user profiles at any time via the Settings console. Permanent deletion purges local SQLite records, synchronized Cloud Firestore nodes, and audit trails in compliance with NIST 800-88.
