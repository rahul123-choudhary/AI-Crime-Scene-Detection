CJIS Standards & Digital Forensics Charter

Privacy Policy & Evidence Governance

REVISION: 2026.4 • COMPLIANCE: CJIS 5.9 / NIST 800-88 • CLASSIFICATION: LAW ENFORCEMENT SENSITIVE

1. Scope and Authorized Custody

The CSI Forensic AI Platform is engineered exclusively for recognized law enforcement agencies, accredited forensic science laboratories, and judicial investigative divisions. All telemetry, crime scene photography, surveillance video feeds, and derived metadata processed by the platform constitute official criminal investigative records subject to strict chain-of-custody protocols.

  • Access is restricted to credentialed law enforcement investigators and laboratory analysts.
  • Row-Level Security (RLS) ensures jurisdictional isolation between police departments and bureaus.
  • Session tokens are cryptographically salted and subject to automated expiration policies.

2. Biometric and Physical Trace Data Handling

Digital evidence containing latent fingerprints, ballistic striations, bloodstain pattern distributions, and facial likenesses are treated as high-integrity forensic artifacts:

  • Raw evidence images are hashed with SHA-256 upon ingestion to establish digital non-repudiation.
  • CLAHE, unsharp masking, and night-vision enhancement layers are stored alongside unedited originals.
  • No evidence or user data is ever licensed, sold, or shared with third-party commercial machine learning models.

3. Evidence Retention, Rotation, and Deletion

Data stored in local SQLite databases and synchronized MongoDB / Firebase cloud instances is retained strictly in accordance with statutory statute-of-limitations schedules:

  • Automated rolling snapshots maintain verifiable restore points with 10-cycle automated rotation.
  • Authorized officers may expunge case dockets or purge individual user accounts in full compliance with NIST 800-88 digital sanitization guidelines.
  • Audit logs capture timestamp, officer badge ID, and IP hash for all create, view, export, and delete actions.

4. Network Security & Threat Rate-Limiting

To defend against unauthorized intrusion and brute-force vector exploration, forensic API endpoints employ active token-bucket rate limiting (Flask-Limiter). Excessive authentication failures trigger temporary IP isolation and notify the security dispatch unit.
